Service–Job Catalog Viewer: UI mockups

Clickable mockups of every page in DESIGN.md §14. They follow the same rules as the real HTML exports: they open by double-click, work offline, and need no server or CDN. Colours, spacing and component anatomy come from the shared assets/tokens.css and assets/components.css, which become the app's theme in M1. The data is the real workbook: every job row from every visible job tab and every master category and service, parsed with the rules in DESIGN.md §8–§10 by scripts/build-mockup-data.mjs. Pages can be checked against the sheet for layout and for data. Every job's drawer shows its sheet and row number.
